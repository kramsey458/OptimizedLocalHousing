## Direction contract

THESIS: Optimized Local Housing is the host's seating chart: every table keeps its number of seats, the children's table is never touched, and the planner moves place cards round in cycles until the whole hall walks the least to where each guest needs to be, even if one guest ends up a little farther. It refuses the Timbermods landing template the old site wore (cream paper, icon tiles, stat tiles, pill badges, eyebrows) and the "everyone right next to work" drawing that contradicted the product.

OWN-WORLD: A planner's board. Light: pinned card stock on a linen-grey board (#e6e1d6 ground, #fbf8f1 card), ink #23201b, plan lines in drafting blue (#2d5f9a) for the arranged plan and madder red (#b4432f) for the plan as it was (the old site's blue-after / orange-before rule, kept and deepened). Dark: the same board by lamplight (#1b1d21 ground, #26292e card, ink #ece6da, blue #8fb6e8, madder #e98a74). Place cards are tented cream cards with a fold line and a hand-lettered name; tables are drawn circles with seat ticks; stations are small labelled plaques. Libre Caslon Text (display, self-hosted, OFL; the face of printed invitations and place cards, chosen over the first pick Fraunces, which the detector flags as overused) for headings and, in italic, the names on cards; body system-ui; log lines in a mono stack on a teletext-strict grid. Flat layered card, no gradients, no drop-shadow stacks beyond one soft pin shadow.

STORY: One look: the colony's total walk, as short as the beds allow, once a day; homes keep their adults, children never move, empty beds are left to the game. Then: why a lottery of beds wastes walking; the pass in five steps with a move cycle drawn as place cards going round; always / never; the honest comparison with Housing Optimize and Commute Balancer; the replay and the live log; what is tested and what isn't; install.
